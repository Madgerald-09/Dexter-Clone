Speak replies aloud

Use a voice provided by Windows and Chromium. This feature does not send audio to a service.

♩
Enable speech input

Voice dictation uses Chromium’s built-in speech recognition and may send audio to its recognition provider. Leave this off for fully local use. The microphone button and Ctrl+Shift+Space hotkey work only when enabled.

◉
Wake phrase: “Hey Dexter”

Optional continuous listening while the app is open. Audio may be processed by Chromium’s recognition provider. Say “Hey Dexter” then a command; Dexter puts the transcript in the box for you to review and send.
